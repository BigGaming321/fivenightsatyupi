# FIVE NIGHTS AT CIRIACOS

Five Nights at Ciriaco’s is a 2D First-person POV survival horror game inspired
by the Five Nights at Freddy’s (FNAF) franchise. The game is set inside a
fictional version of the university campus after hours, where a student stays
overnight on school premises and must survive five terrifying nights.

The university’s antagonistic characters, modeled after campus staff and
familiar characters, come to life at night. Each of them has its own unique
quirks and weaknesses. The student must use a network of security cameras,
limited power reserves, and two lockable office doors to prevent these
characters from reaching his office and hold out until 6:00 AM each night. As
the nights progress, these characters grow more aggressive. Survive all five
nights to escape and win.
